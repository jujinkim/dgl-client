Use case: deterministic geometric application icon.
Asset type: DGL application icon, transparent 1024 x 1024 RGBA master artwork.
Production: Pillow renders the shared M/L/C/Z contours in monochrome.json. This file records the design brief; no image generation service is called.
Primary request: Make the icon say what DGL does: a terminal whose broken connection loops back and resumes.
View: Flat, straight-on symbol. No perspective, visible side faces, bevels, shadows, highlights, or textures on the symbol.
Composition: A landscape ivory terminal window with a thick rounded frame, split by an ivory vertical divider into two tmux panes; the left pane is wider. The frame's top-right corner is cut open. A lime clockwise arc of the same stroke width bridges the opening, starting just after the cut top edge and ending in a lime arrowhead that points down toward the resumed right edge, with small gaps on both ends. Put a lime > prompt and _ cursor in the left pane as >_, and a lime block cursor low in the right pane for the resumed session. Pane interiors are transparent so the background shows through.
Meaning: The window and two panes identify a terminal and tmux workspace. The broken corner that loops back identifies SSH reconnection and tmux reattachment: "Reconnect less. Resume more." Do not use letters, labels, or AI sparkles.
Palette: Ivory #F3EBDD for the frame and divider; lime #C7F43B for the arc, arrowhead, prompt, and cursors. Background is a subtle radial gradient from #232B38 at (50%, 36%) of the tile to #0A0D12 at a 78% radius.
Small sizes: Prioritize the two panes, the lime corner arrow, and >_ at 128, 48, 32, 24, and 16px. Keep strokes thick enough to survive 1px rendering.
Platform output: Render a full-bleed rounded tile at the macOS tile ratio with transparent corners for Windows, MSIX, and the dashboard, directly at each output size, with the symbol scaled to 84% about the tile center for generous padding. Finish the tile edge with an outer dark hairline and an inner white rim that fades from top to bottom; at 32px and below use a single 1px mid-tone rim. Place the same tile inside the existing macOS transparent margin. On Android, use the radial gradient as the adaptive background without a border and fit both color and monochrome artwork inside the same central 54dp circle of the 108dp layer.
